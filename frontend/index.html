Give the agent authority — not your wallet.

An agent that can spend freely is the thing people are right to refuse. This one acts inside limits the shopper signed, and every one of them is checked before money moves.

The agent may

  • Search every shop at once
  • Compare offers and reliability
  • Haggle within the merchant's own floor
  • Choose, and explain the choice
  • Pay — inside the signed limits

The agent may not

  • Spend a rupee over the ceiling
  • Substitute a different kind of product
  • Ignore what was asked for
  • Go below a merchant's price floor
  • Confirm a delivery on the shop's behalf
